01 — Who we are

A manufacturing partner built on systems thinking.

Incorporated in Bengaluru on 4 October 2011, Melstar Manufacturing Systems Private Limited has spent 15 years solving complex manufacturing challenges for industry. We supply precision-engineered components and sub-assemblies, built through CNC machining, fabrication and customised industrial design.

Our registered office is in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru. Production runs from Plot No. 15, KIADB Industrial Area, Obadenahalli, Doddaballapura, where people, process and equipment work as one measured, repeatable system.

02 — Capabilities

What we engineer

Six capabilities. Each part is engineered to drawing, built to specification and checked before it leaves the plant.

CNC Machining

CNC turning and milling to tight tolerances, from prototype quantities to steady production runs.

Fabrication

Cutting, bending, welding and finishing for frames, brackets, enclosures and structural parts.

Precision Components

Precision-engineered parts for industrial equipment, made to drawing and repeatable from batch to batch.

Sub-assemblies

Components integrated into ready-to-fit sub-assemblies, built to documented work instructions and tested before dispatch.

Customised Industrial Design

Design-for-manufacture reviews and custom design support that turn your requirement into a production-ready part.

Quality Inspection

In-process and final inspection with calibrated gauges, recorded results and lot traceability.

Industries we serve

Heavy Machinery

Robust machined and fabricated parts for heavy equipment.

Manufacturing Technology

Components and sub-assemblies for production systems.

Specialized Engineering

Custom-designed parts where standard solutions fall short.

    Deliver

    Protective packing and scheduled dispatch to your line.
